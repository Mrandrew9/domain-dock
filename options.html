How labeling works

Open DomainDock, select + Label beside a tab, and save a memorable label, tags, or note. Find it later by typing any word or #shopping.

Page labels are saved against page URLs (Amazon product links are normalized to their product ID). They remain available when that page is reopened. They do not rename Chrome's actual tabs or inspect webpage content.

Privacy

Everything stays in Chrome's local extension storage. No analytics, external requests, cloud sync, or page-content access. This extension requires the tabs permission to search tab titles and URLs, plus tabGroups and storage for grouping and labels.
